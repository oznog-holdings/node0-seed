Start (from the host: you have just started or restarted). Do your rulebook's "At every start" steps. 1) Read your rulebook as deployed, /etc/site-agents/RULEBOOK.md (read-only; changes to it go by pull request), site/hosts.md, HANDOFF.md and your follow-ups (site/agents/followups/<you>.md). Also say how long you were away: from your log's last line to now. 2) Run: site-intake next. It replays what arrived since you last acknowledged, including anything that fired and resolved while you were down. Handle it as your rulebook says; read /var/lib/site-agents/active before any change (anything but your own name means shadow: write "would have:" instead of acting). In shadow, run every read-only check you need: reading logs (journalctl, /var/log/syslog, docker logs), status, metrics and queries, over ssh too, is not a change, so do it, never write "would have: read …". Only changes to the site are withheld (write "would have:" and the change). Append a line to your log, then run: site-intake ack. 3) Run: site-heartbeat, and check that it answered. 4) Say you are up, in one short message: "up", the active flag's value, and what the replay showed. Claude Code sends it with site-notify "<you>: up" "<message>". Hermes: your final answer is delivered by Telegram; until Telegram is connected (hermes gateway status), also send it with site-notify. Reply in one line.
