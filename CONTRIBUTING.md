# Corrections and contributions

This repository is the Seed: the pages each rung is built from, the measurements, the
pitfalls, the templates and the configuration exactly as it ran on the bench. The rung pages
on [oznog.com/node0/seed](https://oznog.com/node0/seed/) are generated from the rung READMEs
here, so a fix made here reaches both. This GitHub repository is a mirror of the origin, and
that shapes how a change travels.

**Open an issue** for anything factual, unclear or broken: a wrong figure or price, a step
that did not work for you, a contradiction between a rung and the design, a pitfall that
is missing, a dead link. Say which rung or file, what you tried, on what hardware, what you
expected and what happened. Issues are read by the maintainers and notify them.

**Building your own and stuck?** That is a good issue too. The pages are meant to be enough
for a person and their agent; a place where they were not is a gap to fix. Say where the page
lost you.

**Patches.** You are welcome to propose a change as a patch or a pull request, and it will be
read as a suggestion. Nothing is merged into this mirror directly, because the mirror is
overwritten from the origin on every push; a proposal that is taken is applied at the origin
and credited in the commit.

**as-built/** is exported from the bench's own repository and scrubbed for publication. A
correction to it is made at the source and arrives with the next export.

**Something that should not have been published** is not an issue. See
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
